# The Return — Product Series Plan

## The Core Idea
Build a **product ladder** — each sequel goes deeper and costs more. Readers of Book 1 are the warmest audience for Book 2.

---

## The Series Structure

### Book 1 — *The Return: Finding Yourself Again After Heartbreak*
- **Format:** Ebook (25–40 pages)
- **Price:** $17
- **Focus:** The emotional journey — grief, identity, solitude, rebuilding
- **Audience:** Anyone fresh out of a breakup

### Book 2 — *The Standard: How to Know What You Actually Want Next Time*
- **Format:** Ebook + Workbook (journal prompts, self-audit)
- **Price:** $27
- **Focus:** Values, patterns, red flags, what you actually need in a partner
- **Audience:** People who've done the healing work and are ready to think about love again

### Book 3 — *The Real Thing: How to Build a Relationship That Actually Lasts*
- **Format:** Ebook + Worksheets
- **Price:** $37
- **Focus:** Communication, attachment styles, how to love without losing yourself
- **Audience:** People in new relationships or approaching one

---

## The Product Ladder

```
$17  →  The Return       (Entry point — widest audience)
$27  →  The Standard     (Upsell after Book 1)
$37  →  The Real Thing   (Upsell after Book 2)
$97+ →  Community / Coaching  (Top of ladder — most committed buyers)
```

---

## How to Set It Up for Sequels from Day 1

1. **Name the series** — give it a brand ("The Return Series")
2. **End every book** with a teaser for the next one — last page is a soft pitch
3. **Build your email list** from Book 1 buyers — that's your sequel launch audience
4. **Sell Book 1 first** — validate before writing Book 2
