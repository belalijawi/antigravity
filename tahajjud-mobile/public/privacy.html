Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 13, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Tahajjud+ ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Location Information

We request access to your device's precise or approximate location. This data is used solely to calculate accurate local prayer times and determine the Qibla direction relative to your position. This calculation is performed on your device or via ephemeral requests to prayer time services. We do not store your location history.

b. Account Information

If you choose to create an account using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect your Name and Email Address. This information is used to:

  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Synchronize your App data (such as prayer logs, streak history, and personal duas) across your devices via Firebase Cloud Sync.

c. Device and Usage Data

We may use third-party services like Google Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. This helps us identify stability issues and improve the app experience. This data does not personally identify you.

2. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To provide core features (Prayer Times, Qibla, Cloud Sync).
  • Account Management: To maintain your user account and secure your data.
  • Authentication: To verify your identity when you log in.
  • Notifications: To send you prayer reminders and alerts (if opted in).

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted third-party service providers necessary for app functionality. These third parties include:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android functionality and app distribution.
  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, database storage, crash reporting, and analytics.
  • Apple: Used for Apple Sign-In and iCloud synchronization features.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data (Name, Email, Sync Data) as long as you maintain an active account. You have full control over your data.

Requesting Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. To do so, you may:

  • In-App: Use the "Delete Account" option within the app settings (Settings > Account > Delete Account).
  • Email: Contact us directly at tahajjud.letters@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account and personal data from our servers within 30 days. This includes all synced prayer logs, streak history, and personal duas.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If needed, please contact us to remove such information.
