2. What Tahajjud+ does

Tahajjud+ is a religious practice companion for Muslims. It provides:

  • Prayer times based on your location, including mosque timetable import via AI photo scan
  • Qibla direction
  • Tahajjud (night prayer) tracker, streak, journal, and reminders
  • Prayer analytics and weekly review
  • The Quran in 30+ translations with audio from 7 reciters, offline download, and Hifz memorisation mode
  • Tasbeeh (dhikr) counter with session history
  • A dua library with audio, a Night Journal, and Letters to Allah
  • A community Dua Wall, shared Tahajjud Stories, replies/comments on both, and a Global Tahajjud Map
  • An opt-in Leaderboard ranking dhikr, Qur'an-reading, and Tahajjud activity under a nickname you choose
  • Accountability partner circle with push notifications
  • Optional cloud sync, optional premium subscription

The App is for personal spiritual use. It is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified scholar.
