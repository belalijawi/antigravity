Pray in the last third of the night.

The Lord descends to the lowest heaven in the last third of every night and asks who is calling. Tahajjud+ helps you be there.

Built for one thing

Every feature exists to make praying Tahajjud easier to start, sustain, and deepen.

🌙

Smart bedtime

We read your past sleep (with permission) and suggest a bedtime that lands you in the blessed last third — naturally, without dragging yourself awake.

🔔

Stacked reminders

Multiple gentle alarms before Fajr so the first miss doesn't break your night. Live Activities on your Lock Screen count down to the moment.

📖

Madinah Mushaf

The Quran in 30+ verified translations — Tanzil-sourced, scholar-endorsed. Beautiful Amiri Quran typography. Word-by-word audio sync.

🎙️

7 reciters

Alafasy, Sudais, Maher Al-Mueaqly, Saood Ash-Shuraym, Abdul Basit, Minshawi, Husary. Download for offline listening. Full-screen player with sleep timer.

✍️

Night journal

Reflect after every Tahajjud. Log mood, rakats, and the dua on your heart. Look back at flashbacks from a month ago.

🤲

Dua Wall

An anonymous community feed of duas. Publish your own, say Ameen on others', and reply to keep the conversation going — never tied to your name.

✨

Tahajjud Stories

Read real stories of answered duas and changed lives from the community — and share your own when a prayer is answered.

🏆

Leaderboard

Opt in with a nickname and see how your dhikr, Qur'an reading, and Tahajjud nights stack up — weekly and all-time, worldwide or by country.

🧭

Qibla compass

Find the exact direction of the Kaaba from anywhere, instantly — no signal or internet required.

🔥

Streaks that forgive

Weekly freeze — miss a night, keep the streak. Built for sustainability, not pressure.

🧠

Hifz mode

Structured ayah repetition for serious Quran memorization. Progressive locking, review tracking, audio loops.

👥

Accountability Circle

Connect with up to 5 partners. Wake each other for Tahajjud, see who prayed tonight, build a community of night-worshippers.

🌍

Global Tahajjud Map

See anonymous dots of Muslims praying right now around the world. Tap to add your own — city level only, disappears after 24 hours.

📊

Prayer analytics

Weekly breakdown of every prayer, streak history, and your all-time consistency — all stored privately on your device.

🕌

Mosque timetable

Photograph your mosque's printed schedule and AI reads it instantly. Your mosque's exact times, not just calculated ones.

📿

Tasbeeh counter

Count dhikr with haptic feedback, set custom goals, and review session history. Meanings shown for every dhikr.

⌚

Apple Watch + Siri

Log Tahajjud from your wrist. "Hey Siri, log Fajr" from anywhere. Lock Screen widgets show your next prayer and streak.

🎨

Five premium themes

Emerald, Gold, Rose, Purple, Cosmic. Each one is a different mood for a different night.
