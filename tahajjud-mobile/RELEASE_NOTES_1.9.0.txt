LEADERBOARD (new)
See how your dhikr, Qur'an reading and Tahajjud compare with Muslims around the world. This week and all-time, plus a view of people near you. Entirely optional: you pick a nickname, you can add your country, and you can leave at any time.

REPLIES ON THE DUA WALL AND TAHAJJUD STORIES (new)
Reply to someone's dua or story to encourage them, with your name and country shown beside your words.

DUAS ON THE GLOBAL MAP (new)
Pin your dua to the world map so others can find it and pray for you. Tap any pin to read the dua and say Ameen without leaving the map.

ANSWERED DUAS (new)
Mark your dua as answered and share how it was answered. Everyone who said Ameen is told — so they know their prayer mattered.

MY DUAS
Every dua you've published, in one place. Delete any of them at any time, and it comes off the wall and the map straight away.
